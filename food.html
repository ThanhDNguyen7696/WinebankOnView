Dining inside Bendigo history

Our Victorian-era building was constructed in 1876 and was once home to the Union Bank and the Bank of Australasia and New Zealand. Today it brings together local hospitality, quality food and a cellar of Australian and international wines.

Our philosophy is simple: serve distinctive, high-quality food at fair value, with flexible plates made for sharing and wines selected to complement the table.
